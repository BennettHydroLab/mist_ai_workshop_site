About the Workshop

Sediment transport is a critical process influencing riverine and coastal environments, infrastructure sustainability, and water resource management. Despite the unifying principles of sediment transport mechanics, collaboration between fundamental transport researchers and applied engineering experts remains limited. This workshop aims to bridge these gaps by convening experts in sediment transport, AI, and related disciplines.

This workshop is jointly supported by the NSF PMP, ECI, and WaLCZ programs.

The workshop will explore three key themes: 1) enhancing predictive models for sediment dynamics using machine learning; 2) integrating remote sensing and real-time data assimilation with AI; 3) advancing hybrid modeling approaches that combine physical and AI-driven methods. Participants will address pressing engineering challenges including excessive sediment mining, reservoir sedimentation, and sustainable sediment management strategies.
